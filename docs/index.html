Motion Offense Playbook

7th grade girls · 4-out 1-in motion

The decision: 4-out 1-in motion

You get the spacing and cutting of 5-out, and your tall, tough post stays on the block where she helps most. When she subs out, the same rules become 5-out. There's nothing new to teach.

Roles

P

Post (tall, tough)

Lives on the ball-side block. She seals, catches, chins the ball, drop-steps and crashes every shot. No perimeter work for now.

1

Skilled athlete

Starts at the top and does most of the driving. She also flashes to the high post. Driving gaps in motion builds her handle.

2

Fast guards

Play the wings and corners. They score on backdoor cuts and drive-and-kick. Soccer players already get give-and-go.

The 5 rules

Tap Next or tap the court to step through each one.

1

Pass and cut

After you pass, basket-cut hard to the rim with your hand up.

2

Fill the hole

If the cutter isn't open, she clears to the open spot. Everyone rotates. The top is never empty.

3

Dribble at you = backdoor

If a teammate dribbles toward you, cut backdoor to the basket.

4

Overplayed = backdoor

If your defender denies the pass, step out, plant and cut to the rim.

5

Post follows the ball

P stays on the ball side. When the ball changes sides, she cuts low, block to block. She crashes every shot.

Team constraint

Three passes before a jump shot. A layup is always OK.

Against a zone: Use the same spots and rules, but cut into the gaps instead of to the rim. P moves to the short corner behind the zone. A skip pass from one wing to the other is usually open. See
Two lineups, same rules. Big (P + 1 + 3 guards) runs 4-out 1-in. Small (1 + 4 fast guards) runs 5-out and pairs well with your half-court trap.

Game day notes

  • In the first week, praise good cuts and spacing even when the pass doesn't get there.
  • Call "Post!" for a set that gets P a touch with the side cleared out. It's on the Plays tab.
  • Track backdoor layups and post touches each game. Those numbers show whether the offense is working.

The drills are listed in practice order. Each one builds toward the full offense.

0:00 · 10 min

Dynamic warm-up and form shooting

  • Players go baseline to baseline with jogging, high knees, carioca and defensive slides.
  • In pairs, players take one-hand form shots close to the rim and step back as they make them.
  • P shoots only from the blocks, off the glass.
0:10 · 10 min

Ball handling, jump stops and pivots

  • Two-ball stationary dribbling (3 min): pound, alternate and low-high. Pair your skilled girl with a guard.
  • Full-court change of direction (4 min): cross over or go between the legs at each cone, then finish with a layup.
  • Jump stop and pivot (3 min): sprint, catch a self-toss, jump-stop, then front-pivot and reverse-pivot.
Motion breaks down when kids travel or bunch up after the catch. Every catch should be a jump stop, facing the basket.
0:20 · 4 min

2v0 pass and cut

The top player passes to the wing and basket-cuts. The wing hits her for a layup. Switch lines.

0:24 · 4 min

2v0 dribble-at backdoor

The top player dribbles at the wing. The wing plants her outside foot, cuts backdoor and gets a bounce pass.

0:28 · 4 min

3v0 pass, cut and fill

This is the heart of the offense. Top and two wings: pass, cut, clear to the open spot, fill the top. Go nonstop for 5 passes, then a layup.

Split stations (0:32 · 10 min, swap at 5)

Post station

Seal, catch, drop step

P, the skilled girl and one more player. Teach two moves only: drop step to a power layup and drop step to the middle for a jump hook.

Post station

Low cut, block to block

When the ball swings, P cuts under the basket and seals on the new side. Then add the skilled girl flashing to the high post, where she faces up and drives or hits P.

Guard station

1v1 deny to backdoor

The defender denies the wing. The wing steps out, plants and cuts backdoor for a bounce pass from the coach.

Guard station

Closeout 1v1

The coach passes to a wing while her defender closes out. She attacks with two dribbles max and finishes at the rim. Tell the soccer players it's like beating a defender into space.

0:42 · 13 min

Shell 5v0: install the rules

  • 3 min: Rules 1 and 2 only. Freeze the drill and fix the spacing (about 15 ft apart).
  • 3 min: Add Rule 5. P cuts low every time the ball changes sides.
  • 3 min: Add Rules 3 and 4. Coach calls "Dribble at!" or "Deny!" to trigger them.
  • 4 min: Full speed, 5 passes then attack. Then pull P, sub in a guard, and run 5-out.
1:00 · 13 min

Shell 5v3 (advantage)

  • Five on offense, three defenders playing man and denying the passing lanes. The denial sets up backdoors.
  • Scoring: any basket is 1 point. A backdoor layup or post score is 2 points.
  • Rotate three players onto offense each turn. P always plays post on offense and defends the post when she isn't on offense.
  • Freeze the drill when a player stands after passing, two players share a spot, or P drifts away from the ball.
1:13 · 14 min

4v4 constraint scrimmage

  • Rounds 1–2 (6 min): 4-out, no post. Three passes before any shot. A dribble-at must produce a backdoor cut.
  • Rounds 3–4 (8 min): P plays the block for one team and the skilled girl for the other. Winners stay on, and losers run a sprint.
  • If there's time: 2 minutes of your 3-2 zone on defense so the offense sees motion against a zone.
